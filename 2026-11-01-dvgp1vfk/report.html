#Gameplay

60 messages from 4 people

The week in #Gameplay kicked off with dbugslayer checking in on progress after admitting they hadn't finished their own tasks in time for v0.1.2. cpgeiste shared a thorough evaluation of dialogue system options. Behaviour trees were ruled out as too complicated and rigid. Unreal's built-in Common Conversations plugin is a decent fit for simpler multi-NPC conversations, though it needs git LFS locks so only one person edits the conversation tree assets at a time. Data-table approaches like most Fab assets were also considered. Ink came out as the most flexible option: friendly for narrative writers, quick for programmers to learn, and possibly convertible from Twine with generative tools. cpgeiste shared the Inkpot demo (a Drive build plus the The-Chinese-Room/InkpotDemo GitHub source), the WritingWithInk docs, and a tip to explore it in the Inky editor. dbugslayer played through the demo and was impressed that Ink could cover dialogue, journal entries and quests in one go. They floated pausing their work on #142 to help integrate it, and cpgeiste confirmed he had already started integrating Inkpot into the game. dbugslayer encouraged collaborating with narrative and the dialogue pod and test-running the workflow with placeholder dialogue. cpgeiste agreed, stressing that it's a team decision.

The second half of the week was a debugging session. voymasa built an oxygen refill station for issue 187 using BPI_Interact, which lives in Content/Core/Interactables, but kept getting "interaction failed". Xhemoz and voymasa worked through several possible causes: casting to the character, a server-authority icon, and tight coupling with the puzzle logic in BP_Interactable. They also found the oxygen component isn't on BP_AtlantisPlayerCharacter; it's reached through GetOxygenComponent on the player state. Their print strings never fired, which suggests the blueprint isn't being hit at all. cpgeiste suggested turning on the trace's Draw Debug Type and adjusting the station's collision profile so it blocks the trace channel. dbugslayer suggested breakpoints and noted the interaction system is finicky right now (issues 382 and 383).
