Atomech Observer terms of service

Effective 9 November 2026.

The service

Atomech Observer is a free Discord bot that writes weekly newsletters for the Funsmith Club and ASGC Game Jam servers. It is offered as is, with no guarantee that it will be available or that its summaries are complete or accurate. Summaries are written by AI and can contain mistakes.

Who can use it

The bot runs only in servers its operator adds it to. Its commands are limited to server members with the Manage Server permission. Using it in a server means agreeing to these terms and to Discord's own Terms of Service.

Your content

Messages you post in channels the bot can read may be summarized, quoted or credited by name in the newsletter, as described in the privacy policy. You keep ownership of what you post. You can ask to be left out of future newsletters, or to have your stored messages deleted, by contacting support@gamedesignskills.com.

Acceptable use

Don't use the bot to break Discord's rules or the law, or to try to get data about people the bot was not set up to summarize.

Changes and ending the service

These terms or the bot may change or stop at any time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
