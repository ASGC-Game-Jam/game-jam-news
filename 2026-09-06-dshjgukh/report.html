Pods spin up, docs get simpler, and the Alpha gets pushed back: a week of getting unblocked

1202 messages · 107 active members · 50 active channels

The server was busy from Aug 30 to Sep 6, with 1,202 messages from 107 members. Most of the energy went into feature pods organizing themselves. Investigation (FDS-005) was the busiest channel. Anrakyr and binarie stepped up to co-lead design, pending Matt Barnes' sign-off, and consolidated the FDS. Andrea's tech session concluded that Discover is the only real mechanic. The merge process was kept deliberately light: "it's a game jam, not airplane or heart surgery software." Xandemon's greybox UI menu flow (PR #126) was merged. Much of the week went to onboarding: getting people through asgcgithub.pulli.me, fixing Gitea logins and an outage, and walking artists and designers through the Visual Studio toolchain needed to build the Unreal project. Carlos Ortiz's "Don't reinvent the wheel" reminder sparked a lively thread on Unreal's thin documentation.

Art kept pace. New Technical Art, 3D Environment and 3D Character channels launched. Anki pushed underwater post-processing and an oxygen tank prototype, and Smetch_D pushed a city center blockout. The concept art second pass brought pearl divers, an anglerfish archaeologist couple and VarunP's whale_shop concept. In narrative, a theme winner emerged while premises stayed split, and the Game Writing pod kicked off a Steampunk Tech Bible. Audio iterated on the Project Atlantis menu theme. With PAX, SLICE, Gamescom recaps and a long weekend, many leads were away. Members covered for each other, and the Saturday producer call moved to async stand-ups.
