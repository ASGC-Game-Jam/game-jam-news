Weekly Digest

Server report: Sep 06 – Sep 13, 2026

First Playable gets a date (Oct 10) as pods push past ClickUp chaos and PR #158 lays the code foundation

1293 messages · 119 active members · 48 active channels

This was a busy organizing week for the ASGC Game Jam 2026 crew: 1,293 messages from 119 active members. The biggest news came out of #🎯│director-discussion. After a prioritization session with Matt Barnes and VibeBlind (Seth), Cafedunoir announced a First Playable target for the end of Sprint 6 (October 10th). Its scope covers Player Character, Traversal (swimming, ballast, walking), the Oxygen System, and groundwork for the Investigation System. On the code side, Carlos Ortiz's foundational PR #158 landed. It sets up Atlantis___ class naming, C++ base classes, reparented Blueprints and docs, and Xandemon approved and merged it to give every pod a baseline. Carlos followed up with a heavily reacted call for everyone to go find or create tickets rather than wait to be assigned. VibeBlind published first-draft TDS specs as GitHub issues for Investigation, Journal and Dialogue.

The feature pods kept tightening their alignment. A Sep 7 Investigation/Journal/Dialogue cross-pod sync settled the handoffs between them: no Investigation-specific progress UI, Dialogue flags feeding the Journal's internal monologue, and a target of about 6 hours of play. David "AssassinCS" Thompson organized a Sep 10 cross-pod Oxygen alignment meeting. It proposed removing passive drain, adding a meditation mechanic to regenerate oxygen, making normal combat actions cost 0 oxygen, and having stealth allocate a percentage of oxygen on entry. Art and tech art stayed busy too: sevenn4's underwater visuals branch, three sand material variants and a Substance Designer template; Brandon Trabon's player character sculpt; VarunP's whale shop interior concept; and LockedInFish's tutorial cave music, which went to V2 the same day.

Process friction was a constant. ClickUp permissions broke the async stand-up, so Cafedunoir moved future stand-ups to Discord threads, and David traced the root cause to how Limited Members see spaces. Quest Design spent the whole week without a confirmed Design point of contact, and several pods (District Design, Resource Economy, NPC Interactions) remain blocked waiting on Narrative's high-level plot outline, which is due at the end of the sprint. On a lighter note, AaronBurrSir opened a Steam key request thread that drew about 60 requests, and Lianhua kicked off a set of get-to-know-you threads on favorite books, games, shows and music.
