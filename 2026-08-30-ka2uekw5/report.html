Server report: Aug 23 – Aug 30, 2026

Docs move to GitHub, themes get their top 3, and the jam pushes from planning toward prototyping

1175 messages · 131 active members · 48 active channels

This week (Aug 23–29) the ASGC Game Jam 2026 server was busy, with 1,175 messages from 131 active members. Most of the energy went into turning feature designs into buildable work. VibeBlind (Seth) drafted Technical Design Docs as GitHub issues for nearly every pod: Traversal #22, Oxygen #23, Journal #24, Investigation #26, Combat #28, Stealth #29, Resource Economy #30 and Dialogue #115. Pods were asked to break these down into TDS child issues. Fefly shipped the first Journal TDS (#116), and Oxygen pod members underwolf and Josue Monge filled out the technical and asset sections of FDS-003. In #tech-lead-discussion and elsewhere, people pushed back that there was too much documentation before any doing. Xandemon called for graybox testing, VibeBlind called the docs "more an obstacle more than tool," and the guidance shifted to prototyping now, locking assets, opening PRs and "just don't break main."

On the creative side, Narrative ran a voice-session review of theme pitches. A new Theme Refinement pod then delivered three final themes on Aug 29: cooperation across differences, accepting uncomfortable truths, and responsible progress. Premise 2, a journalist covering an underwater city as it opens up, gained momentum and goes to Diana's directors meeting on Tuesday. Art reorganized around Zef's lead/PoC structure and opened a Sprint 2.5 concept pass with crab buskers, Crabengers, Squidfolk and character sheets. Artists also made Unreal experiments with caustics and bubble shaders. Diana Donado clarified that PoCs relay information rather than call the shots.

Operations matured as well. Directors settled on a 50/50 gameplay/narrative split with no voice-over. Felix launched a ClickUp hygiene bot and a sprint analytics tool. The Steam build pipeline now works end to end with a clear release role split. Weekly Director, Production and QA syncs are on the calendar ahead of Sprint 4, which starts Aug 30. Members also flagged loose ends: Investigation lost its lead, several pods still need producers or PoCs, and the FAQ and NDA thread disagree on public sharing.
