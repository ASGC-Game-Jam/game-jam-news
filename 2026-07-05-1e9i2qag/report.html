Deputies began collecting each department's tools and drafting risk mitigation and communication plans. Design set outline docs due by the 10th.

The unofficial lean is now Unreal 5.7.4, with an ADR in progress. With the US July 3–4 holiday, several teams pushed deadlines and meetups to next week.

Last week it goes through the final spec review, estimates and allocation before entering the dev sprint. We need to compress these timelines based on: skill and resources available for the dev work (for various departments) and our timebox of 6 months of this game jam.

Epics to Leads happen during sprint planning that is led by the producers. You have Brief approval through game pillars -> GDD creation, review and scoping -> TDD and estimates -> Sprint planning and sprint allocation, that's when the Epic is created, assigned to a sprint and sent to the department lead. If a task has dependencies, it is assigned to the different departments in the order of dependency resolution. For example: can't implement in code, till art is made, can't create art till designer defines the characteristics/theme of the visual.
