Weekly Digest: Full Steam Ahead, Under the Sea

Weekly Digest · Jul 05 – Jul 12, 2026 · 586 messages from 45 members

What a week, leads! The ASGC Community Game Jam 2026 crew traded 586 messages across 45 members, and the game finally has a shape: an underwater steampunk mystery built in Unreal 5.7.4. With signups closing July 14 and the jam kicking off July 20, here's everything you need to catch up.

🔒 The Vision Is Locked

On Wed Jul 8, shadowarrior announced in #leads-discussion that every first-milestone decision is locked (+38 reactions):

  • Environment/Setting: Underwater, Steampunk
  • Gameplay: Adventure & Exploration, with "exploring new worlds and people" as the fun factor
  • Player feel: Clever and Cozy
  • Story: Mystery/Detective/Thriller
  • Music: Fantasy (think Elder Scrolls, Dragon Age)
  • Tech: Stylized 3D, humanoid characters, Unreal 5.7.4, PG, Steam on Windows PC

This unblocks Tech to set up the dev environment and lets Analytics prepare the Game Analytics SDK integration. Before the lock, Zef's synthesis of the scattered design docs (elevator pitch, four pillars, and a core loop of "go down, get stuff, make/improve stuff, use new stuff, repeat but better") got everyone onto the same page. MisterLaddie also clarified that Unreal is just the tool, not a goal to make an "Unreal game."

Still open: camera perspective. exackley raised it in #design-lead-discussion, and Matt Barnes said it isn't settled; an investigation game could use a mix. In #Project Alignment, VibeBlind questioned whether combat, stealth, traversal, dialogue and the journal/clues system can all fit in 6 weeks. shadowarrior will define an MVP with Diana Donado, and suggested text dialogue with short constructed-language clips instead of recorded voice.

🛠️ Tech: Branches, Ownership & Friendshipper

In #tech-lead-discussion and #tech-discussions, VibeBlind led planning for an expected ~60 contributors, tapering to about 15 within roughly 3 months.

Branch model: trunk-based, with a protected main, no dev branch, short-lived feature/<task> branches and temporary release/<milestone> branches.

Task ownership: each task is about a workday of effort. Xelnath's rule: finish it, get help, or raise a white flag within 24 hours.

Cadence: alternating dev and stabilization weeks were floated to pair with Sidds' 1-week/2-week QA teams.

Merge conflicts: Carlos Ortiz pushed modular architecture, dependency management and regular rebases.

Tool roles: dyzzy and Adrian will co-own Tools.

Friendshipper: dyzzy, klight and Carlos debugged it, but it depends on AWS, SSO, GitHub merge queues and Argo. That makes it a poor fit for our Gitea VPS and no-budget setup. Ark VCS was mentioned as an alternative.

Storage: the VPS has 38GB with about 3GB used. Adrian will expand it at 50% use and suggested cheap S3 storage (~6 EUR/TB/month) for builds.

Tech Sync pt2: recorded with Fireflies. Carlos demoed a tool everyone loved and plans to put it on live servers for testing before the 20th.

Onboarding resources: VibeBlind proposed a UE5 version-control YouTube video as the contributor tutorial, preferring .gitattributes by file extension. He also shared C++ resources, including Tom Looman's guide and Epic's tutorials.

Risk plan: in #Production Deputy Leads, Felix delivered a copy-paste-ready risk doc:

  • 48h warning trigger for inactive tasks
  • 72h trigger to strip the task
  • Plan B: hand off to a buddy or post to a #critical-bounties channel
