This was a heavy organizing week for the ASGC Community Game Jam 2026 leadership team, with 586 messages from 45 members. The biggest moment came on Wed Jul 8, when shadowarrior announced in #leads-discussion that all first-milestone decisions are locked (+38 reactions). The game will be an underwater steampunk adventure/exploration game aiming for a clever, cozy feel and a mystery/detective story. It will use Stylized 3D rendering and humanoid characters, and it is built in Unreal 5.7.4, rated PG, for Steam on Windows PC. That unblocked Tech to set up the dev environment and Analytics to prepare SDK integration. Zef's synthesis of the scattered design docs (elevator pitch, four pillars, core loop) helped pull everyone onto the same page. Xelnath set the overall arc: a Steam-testable build by July 21, playtests from July 25, a feedback pipeline by July 28, something playable by end of August, and a big sit-down in early October.

Production confirmed ClickUp as the source of truth, and by Jul 10 Cafedunoir and shadowarrior had sprints and a task board running. Cafedunoir finalized the Project Milestones and Timeline in the Master Game Direction Doc. Deputy producers delivered the risk and communication drafts, and Felix produced a copy-paste-ready risk doc with 48h/72h inactivity triggers. Attention is now shifting to the public onboarding checklist ahead of the jam timeline: signups close July 14, holding room invites go out July 17, and the jam starts July 20.

The tech leads, led by VibeBlind, spent the week on a trunk-based branch model, task ownership rules (about a workday of effort, with a 24-hour 'white flag' rule) and planning for an expected ~60 contributors. They also evaluated Friendshipper and concluded its AWS and GitHub dependencies make it a poor fit for the Gitea VPS and no-budget setup. On the creative side, Sabian and Diana started Miro mood boards, coral-inspired and Art Nouveau ideas came up, Atlantis emerged as a tonal north star, and narrative and design teams scheduled their first official meetings. Scope came up as a real concern: VibeBlind questioned whether the planned features fit in 6 weeks, and shadowarrior committed to defining an MVP with Diana Donado.
