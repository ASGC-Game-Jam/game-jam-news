Server report: Nov 01 – Nov 08, 2026

Gitea breach cleaned up, Alpha 2 locked to end of November, and the world finally gets a walkable spine from tutorial to hub to Puzzle 1

1276 messages · 85 active members · 64 active channels

It was a busy week for the ASGC jam: 1,276 messages from 85 active members. The biggest news was a security incident. Adrian found that the ASGC Gitea VPS had been breached on Nov 4 through a Gitea vulnerability, likely CVE-2026-59774, via a dormant 'it-audit' account created Sept 11. The injected script targeted iPhones on iOS 18.4–18.6 to steal crypto wallet and WhatsApp/Telegram data. Adrian wiped the server, restored it from clean backups, removed the rogue admins and regenerated the GitHub OAuth key. Everyone needs to log in to Gitea again and set a new password, and dbugslayer recommends enabling 2FA. Anyone who opened asgc.freemyip.com on an affected iPhone during that window should assume they were compromised.

On the build side, the 11/7 Tech Sync set Alpha 2 for the end of November: two playable puzzle rooms connected through the hub. dbugslayer, the week's top poster, did a lot of the heavy lifting. He scaffolded L_Atlantis (PR #500) so you can now walk from the tutorial to the hub to Puzzle 1, landed the grouped-clue journal (#142), enabled in-editor Unreal asset locking via Git LFS 2, set up the new app icons, and offered to implement Puzzle 1 himself for build 0.1.4. Xhemoz merged the hub greybox (#476), and Ö...Ü stepped up with a top-down hub layout (#490). cpgeiste got an Ink/Inkpot dialogue proof of concept hot-reloading in Unreal. The team plans to keep Narrative in Twine for now, possibly with a Twine-to-Ink bridge later. QA (ThePokePreston, Hamm, NamasteGeek) smoke tested 0.1.3, filed bugs #472, #473, #511 and #519, and agreed on a workflow: tickets move through tech review, merge, build, then QA acceptance-criteria checks before Done.

On the creative side, the Characters and Cinematics pod pushed bios and first-meeting scenes into the narrative-twine repo ahead of its Nov 7 target. Lianhua drafted all of the Act 1 main quests, and her naming polls sparked a fun etymology discussion. Art kept iterating under Zef: Nick's copper pipe set, NPC concepts (Sir Richard Black, Dr. Longshore, Seayssal, King Lareas), rocks, seagrass, a hermit crab and the bleaching FX. A new cross-discipline UI/UX push kicked off to build a shared style guide, with an open debate between a modern helmet-HUD look and cozy underwater steampunk. Xelnath confirmed the jam officially ends January 31, 2027, and set out what 'done' means for each vertical. Gameplay is the main gate and needs Diana's sign-off. Several people stepped away for life reasons this week, and Jake's 'real life comes first' summed up how the community responded.
