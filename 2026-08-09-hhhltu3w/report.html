Server report: Aug 02 – Aug 09, 2026

Pods take shape, process gets defined: Week 2 of the ASGC Game Jam is all pre-production, permissions and the first Steam build

3996 messages · 190 active members · 99 active channels

This was a week of organizing nearly 400 volunteers into a working structure. Feature pods for Traversal, Oxygen, Investigation, Journal, Dialogue, Combat and Stealth launched across the server, each filling in Points of Contact for Design, Tech, Production, QA, Narrative, Art and Audio. Early friction over first-come-first-served sign-ups and pods forming on short notice led VibeBlind, Oldladies7, ThePokePreston and others to agree on a pipeline in #production-lead-discussion: Idea > Brief > Director Review > GDD > FDS/pods > TDD > Tasks. David "AssassinCS" Thompson turned that into two docs, 'Feature Planning: From Idea to Pod' and 'Pod Lifecycle: Formation to Closure', plus a ClickUp pod tracker. Cafedunoir approved them as interim process pending a Directors review. The message repeated in #general, #design-questions and #tech-questions was that the project is still in pre-production: pods exist to turn feature docs into TDDs, and implementation comes later.

There were concrete wins too. Ricchio published weekly build 0.0.1 on Aug 4, Xandemon pushed it to Steam within minutes, and AaronBurrSir started distributing keys to testers. Builds will now go to GitHub and Steam every Tuesday. Art lead Zef kicked off Sprint 2 concept briefs for NPCs, the city plaza, props and the playable character, and posted Preliminary Concept Guidelines. Narrative moved into new pod workzones, renamed its pod to the Plot Pod, and scheduled its first live Writer's Room for Sunday Aug 9. Audio posted its first title theme sketches in #Audio-WIP.

Creative direction was debated heavily as well. The 'cozy' pillar was tested against a Zelda-scale combat doc, and the group agreed combat should be optional and non-lethal. The beloved clockwork manta ray journal companion was ruled out of scope because of art staffing, and a dive-suit radio with a voice-only research team took its place. Stealth leaned toward octopus-style camouflage. Leadership turnover shaped much of the week: two of three EP-level leads have left, Yoseph stepped away for a personal emergency, and Amir Satvat announced he is leaving the jam server. Producers and leads stepped up, and GreenCat worked through a long backlog of permission requests.

Themes

  • Defining the pod process: from idea to TDD — Pods started forming before pre-production was finished, which caused confusion and tension over sign-ups. The leads settled on a gated pipeline: no implementation without a TDD, and no TDD without approved design. They wrote interim docs and a ClickUp tracker, standardized the pod name format, and agreed that only the 6–7 PoCs per pod are needed for now. VibeBlind set out what Tech needs from each feature: subfeatures, minimum deliverables, acceptance criteria, dependencies and milestone targets.
